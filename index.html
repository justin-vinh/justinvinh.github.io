About

Who I Am

I'm an immuno-oncology researcher at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, working in the Lindvall & Rhee Labs under Drs. Charlotta Lindvall and John Rhee. I graduated from the University of Virginia in 2025 with a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering (Highest Distinction) and a Minor in Data Science, with a focus on immunoengineering — and a 3.90 GPA.

My research sits at the intersection of translational medicine and artificial intelligence. At Dana-Farber, I leverage LLMs for clinical free text analysis and serious illness care. My prior work at UVA and the National Cancer Institute spanned focused ultrasound immunotherapy, single-cell multiplex imaging, and machine learning approaches to characterizing the tumor microenvironment.

Outside research, I'm a freelance photographer of over a decade, co-founder of Project Ray, and a committed humanitarian volunteering at free medical clinics and homeless ministries. I play guitar, chase clear skies for astrophotography, and remain endlessly fascinated by the cells that hunt cells.

Research

Selected Projects

Dana-Farber Cancer Institute 2025 – Present

Large Language Models for Clinical Free Text Extraction & Serious Illness Care

Lindvall & Rhee Labs
PI: John Rhee, MD, MPH & Charlotta Lindvall, MD, PhD

At Dana-Farber's Lindvall & Rhee Labs, I leverage fine-tuned LLMs to conduct GWAS studies on treatment side-effect data extracted from clinical notes. I also execute key components of a novel clinical trial evaluating LLMs for summarizing serious illness conversations — enrolling patients, conducting interviews, and running downstream statistical analyses.

Clinical AI NLP Oncology GWAS
University of Virginia 2022 – 2023

Focused Ultrasound & Dendritic Cell Immunotherapy

Sheybani Lab
PI: Natasha Sheybani, PhD

Investigated how focused ultrasound (FUS) modulates dendritic cell activity to enhance oncolytic immunotherapies. Characterized extracellular vesicles released by dendritic cells pre- and post-FUS exposure, and developed a novel cost-accessible in vitro FUS platform to make these studies more broadly available to researchers.

Focused Ultrasound Dendritic Cells Immunotherapy EVs Poster ↗
University of Virginia 2023 – 2025

5-ALA Sonodynamic Therapy in Glioblastoma

Sheybani Lab
PI: Natasha Sheybani, PhD

Characterized the effects of 5-aminolevulinic acid (5-ALA) sonodynamic therapy on cancerous glioma stem cells and their secreted extracellular vesicles, with the goal of optimizing treatment-induced cell death and understanding downstream immunological signaling in the tumor microenvironment.

Sonodynamic Therapy Glioblastoma 5-ALA EVs Poster ↗
National Cancer Institute 2023

Multiplex Immunofluorescence & Machine Learning for Tumor Microenvironment Analysis

Center for Biomedical Informatics & Information Technology
PI: Noemi Kedei, MD; Scott Lawrence, MS; Janelle Cortner, PhD

As a 2023 NIH Summer Intern at the NCI Center for Biomedical Informatics, I developed and optimized a NIDAP cloud workflow to analyze single-cell immunofluorescent imagery. Created new graphing modalities and optimized clustering algorithms to detect immune cell phenotypes — advancing democratization of multiplex single-cell analyses for the broader research community.
